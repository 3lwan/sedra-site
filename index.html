Sedra

Every first bite, remembered.

Sedra is a calm, beautiful journal for your baby's first year of solids. Track the foods they discover, note how each one went and keep the photos and funny moments in one place.

  • Follow the "100 foods before 1" challenge, one tap at a time
  • Log every reaction, note and photo
  • See which allergen groups have been tried
  • Get ideas for what to try next
  • Celebrate milestones, all the way to 100

No account. No tracking. Your baby's memories stay on your device.
