Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Sedra works without an account, and everything stays on your device.

How do I log a food?

Open the Foods tab, tap a food and choose "Mark as tried". Pick how it went, add a note or photo if you like, then tap Save. To log the same food again, open it and tap "Log another try".

Do I have to reach 100 foods?

Not at all. "100 foods before 1" is a fun parenting challenge, not a medical requirement. Every baby explores at their own pace.

Does Sedra give medical or allergy advice?

No. Sedra is a memory journal. The allergen groups only show what your baby has tried. Always follow your paediatrician's or health visitor's advice when introducing foods, especially common allergens.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one brings Sedra's data with it. You can also keep a copy any time with Settings → Export data.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete all data. This permanently removes your baby's profile and every food memory from the app.
